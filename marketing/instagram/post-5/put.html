6 ways to use ‘put’ that confuse learners
Same verb. Completely different meanings.
Phrasal Verb · 01
put off
Sense 1
to postpone
“We had to put off the meeting until next week.”
Sense 2
to make someone lose interest
“His rude attitude really put me off.”
Phrasal Verb · 02
put up with
to tolerate something unpleasantInseparable
“I don’t know how she puts up with all that noise.”
Phrasal Verb · 03
put down
Sense 1
to criticize or humiliate someone
“He always puts me down in front of others.”
Sense 2
to set something down
“She put down her book and looked up.”
Phrasal Verb · 04
put out
Sense 1
to extinguish
“Firefighters put out the blaze.”
Sense 2
to inconvenience someone
“I hope I’m not putting you out by asking.”
Phrasal Verb · 05
put through
Sense 1
to connect someone (on the phone)
“Let me put you through to the manager.”
Sense 2
to make someone endure something
“They put her through a tough interview.”
Phrasal Verb · 06
put away
to store something in its proper place
“Please put away your clothes.”
