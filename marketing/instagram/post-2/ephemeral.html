ephemeral
/ɪˈfem.ər.əl/
lasting for a very short time
The Etymology
From the Greek ‘ephemeros’ — ‘lasting but one day,’ from ‘epi’ (on) + ‘hemera’ (day). When it first appeared in English in the late 1500s, it was a medical term for fevers that lasted a single day.
The Deeper Layer
The same Greek root gives us ‘ephemera’ — the word for collectible things never meant to last: old tickets, postcards, programs. A word for the fleeting, applied to the things we keep anyway.
